# Foil Studio 0.1.54 (58)

Prepared on 9 October 2026 for Google Play production.

## Changes since 0.1.39

- Fixed a timeout near the end of cutting that could put the plotter into Hold while it was still moving. Cutting now continues waiting for the controller's acknowledgement while fresh position reports show movement.
- Improved held jogging, stopping and manual Print & Cut alignment, including perimeter capture.
- Added a local project and artwork library with autosave and clearer Open and Import actions.
- Added Android printing with actual-size tiling or page fitting, and a Printer destination for individual objects.
- Added compact rulers, grid snapping and measurement units.
- Improved importing SVG files exported by Foil Studio.
- Artwork can be edited outside the mat with visible warnings before job preparation.
- Improved clipart categories, Cut review panels and phone layouts.

## Physical verification

The cutting fix completed three consecutive runs of a centred 120 x 100 mm
rectangle with the knife removed. Final controller replies took 13.0-13.2
seconds; all three runs completed without a timeout or Hold.

Google Play availability depends on review and rollout. Existing users receive
the update through Google Play when it is approved and available for their device.
