# Foil Studio 0.1.76 — internal billing test

Status: candidate 0.1.76 (80) is being prepared in the internal test track.
It has not replaced the public Google Play production release. Live purchase
validation is still in progress.

Foil Studio introduces three access levels:

- **Free:** design and edit artwork, import files, preview Box wizard and Craft
  wizard projects, and keep one automatic recovery draft.
- **Basic unlock:** a one-time purchase enables named project saves, the project
  library, export, printing and production plotter jobs.
- **Pro:** a monthly or yearly subscription includes Basic features while active
  and enables creating and regenerating projects with Box wizard and Craft wizard.
  A separately purchased Basic unlock remains available after Pro ends.

Prices are supplied by Google Play in the user's local currency. The planned
starting prices are EUR 9.99 once for Basic, EUR 2.99 per month or EUR 24.99 per
year for Pro; availability and displayed regional prices are determined by Play.

Use **Restore purchases** with the same Google Play account to refresh access.
Google Play handles payment details and subscription management. The application
keeps signed purchase information locally; it does not upload artwork for billing.
Verified Basic access can remain available offline. Pro requires periodic online
verification, with a maximum 72-hour offline window after a verified refresh.

During this internal validation, the designated licence tester must use only
Google's test payment methods. Internal track membership alone does not prevent
real charges. Live payment acceptance is being validated and is not yet claimed
complete.

The app includes updated purchase disclosures and preserved third-party notices.
Existing projects remain on the device when updating; uninstalling or clearing
storage can remove private project data.
