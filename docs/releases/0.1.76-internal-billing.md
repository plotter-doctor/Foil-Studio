# Foil Studio 0.1.76 — internal billing test

Status: version 0.1.76 (81) was published to the internal test track on
17 October 2026. Play Console reports it available to internal testers.
On 17 October, the same build was submitted for production review. See the
[public release and previous-buyer claim instructions](0.1.76.md) for current
release information. This page preserves the earlier internal-test record. Core
live purchase flows passed with Google’s no-charge test instruments; extended
acceptance remains partial.

Foil Studio introduces three access levels:

- **Free:** design and edit artwork, import files, preview Box wizard and Craft
  wizard projects, and keep one automatic recovery draft.
- **Basic unlock:** a one-time purchase enables named project saves, the project
  library, export, printing and production plotter jobs.
- **Pro:** a monthly or yearly subscription includes Basic features while active
  and enables creating and regenerating projects with Box wizard and Craft wizard.
  A separately purchased Basic unlock remains available after Pro ends.

The products are active in Google Play. Verified starting consumer prices are:

| Purchase | Poland | Germany |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Basic unlock, once | PLN 44.99 | EUR 9.99 |
| Pro monthly | PLN 12.99 | EUR 2.99 |
| Pro yearly | PLN 109.99 | EUR 24.99 |

Google Play supplies the price shown in your local currency; regional taxes and
rounding vary. There is no introductory trial or prepaid offer.

Use **Restore purchases** with the same Google Play account to refresh access.
Google Play handles payment details and subscription management. The application
keeps signed purchase information locally; it does not upload artwork for billing.
Verified Basic access can remain available offline. Pro requires periodic online
verification, with a maximum 72-hour offline window after a verified refresh.

During this internal validation, the designated licence tester must use only
Google's test payment methods. Internal track membership alone does not prevent
real charges. Live checks passed approved, declined and pending purchases; monthly and yearly
Pro; renewal, cancellation and expiry; account hold and recovery; Basic/Pro
refund and revocation; purchase restoration; and short offline restarts.
Separate-device restore remains pending, along with live clock/72-hour boundary
and grace-specific recovery checks. These are not represented as completed tests.

Build 81 fixes an error after saving or generating a wizard project when no
recovery draft exists. Saving and creating/regenerating Box and Craft projects
were rechecked on the phone. The app includes updated purchase disclosures and
preserved third-party notices.
Existing projects remain on the device when updating; uninstalling or clearing
storage can remove private project data.
